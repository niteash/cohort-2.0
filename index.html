About Us

The Cherry Store Story

Founded in 2009, Cherry Store began as a small family business with a vision to bring the finest Indian fashion to Myanmar. Today, we are one of the leading wholesale suppliers of sarees, kurtis, and Indian grocery products.

Our mission is to empower local retailers with high-quality, authentic Indian products at competitive wholesale prices. We believe in building lasting partnerships based on trust, quality, and mutual growth.
